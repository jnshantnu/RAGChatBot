# Policy Switch Guide: Upgrade, Cross-Grade, Downgrade

## Upgrade

Moving to a higher-tier SKU within the same product line (e.g. AutoCAD
Standard to AutoCAD Pro). Upgrades are effective immediately and trigger
a prorated charge for the remainder of the current term at the new
SKU's price. No approval is required.

## Cross-Grade

Moving to a different product line of equivalent tier (e.g. AutoCAD to
Revit at the same license level). Cross-grades require the customer to
have an active, non-trial subscription and are only permitted once per
term. A prorated adjustment is applied if list prices differ between
the two SKUs.

## Downgrade

Moving to a lower-tier SKU or product line. Downgrades never take effect
immediately -- they are scheduled for the start of the next renewal
term, and no refund is issued for the difference in the current term.
This prevents customers from downgrading late in a term purely to avoid
a true-up charge.

## Term Extensions

None of the above operations change the subscription's renewal date
except where explicitly noted; only a full cancel-and-repurchase resets
the term anniversary.
