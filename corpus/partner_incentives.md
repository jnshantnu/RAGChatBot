# Partner Incentives and Commission Structure

This document is restricted to principal-level partner users. Reseller
and sub-partner accounts are not entitled to commission-rate detail,
even for their own orders.

## Agency Commission Rates

- Standard tier: 15% of net invoice value
- Volume tier (100+ active seats): 20% of net invoice value
- New-logo bonus: additional 5% for the first term of a net-new customer

## Buy-Sell Margin Guidance

Buy-Sell partners do not receive a commission; guidance suggests a
target resale margin of 25-35% over vendor cost, but partners set final
pricing themselves.

## Payout Schedule

Commissions accrue monthly and are paid net-45 after the invoice is
paid in full by the end customer. Commission on an order is clawed back
in full if the underlying order is cancelled or refunded within 90 days.
